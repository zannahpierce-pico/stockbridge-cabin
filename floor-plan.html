Future bathroom for taping out on the floor

The bath as it is drawn in the model, enlarged. Same way up as the floor plan: back wall at the bottom, bank wall on the right. These are design numbers, so they are in black; the red door and window B2 are where the photos put them.

  1. Side wall: snap a line 6'-10" (82") from the bank wall. That is its entry face; the wall is 3½" thick, so the bath is 6'-6" (78") wide inside.
  2. Cabinet wall: its bath face is 4'-11½" (59½") from the back wall and its living-room face 5'-11½" (71½") (it is 12" deep). The bath is 4'-11½" (59½") deep inside.
  3. Door: a 2'-2" (26") rough opening in the side wall, from 2'-8" (32") to 4'-10" (58") off the back wall, for a prehung 24" door hinged on the shower side and swinging in.
  4. Shower: 2'-8" (32") square in the corner by the red door. From its edge to the vanity is 2'-4" (28"); from its front to the cabinet wall is 2'-3" (27").
  5. Toilet: centre line 3'-5" (41") from the back wall, which leaves 1'-3½" (15½") to the vanity and 1'-7" (19") to the cabinet wall. The usual minimum is 15" each side of the centre line, and 21" clear in front.
  6. Things to look at while it is taped out: the side wall lands about ~2" from the edge of the red door (is there room for the door trim?), the door swings across the front of the shower, and the vanity sits under window B2.
