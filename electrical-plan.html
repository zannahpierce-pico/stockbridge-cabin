Power and circuits six 15 A circuits from a battery

The "panel" is an Anker SOLIX 120 V transfer switch on the porch wall, behind the painting, fed by the battery on the floor below it. Its wire whip ends in a junction box just underneath, and the six circuits start there. It is 120 V only, and the battery can supply about 2,400 W in total, so the limit is the battery, not the breakers. Cable runs are measured off the 3D model, where each circuit is drawn in its own colour.

Still to settle

  1. Fixtures. The dome lights, the outdoor sconces and the loft wall light are not chosen yet, so their prices are placeholders.
  2. LED tape and drivers. Assumed 24 V tape with a dimmable driver for each group. The dimmer has to suit the driver: buy them as a matched set.
  3. Counter outlets. The three counter and bar outlets are mounted sideways in the strip between the counter and the window sills. That strip is only about 4" high: measure it before the boxes go in.
  4. Grounding. Check whether the battery bonds neutral to ground, and whether a ground rod is wanted for the outdoor outlets, with whoever does the wiring.
  5. Alarms. Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms are in the materials list but not yet placed on the plan.
