What changed in this revision

  • Under the loft: 12-volt LED tape in two places, all on the dimmer on the cabinet wall at the kitchen end of the couch (S4): facing down onto the books: under the joist above the bank-wall bookshelf, and under the top and the middle shelf of the cabinet wall's book cubbies, so both rows of books are lit. The tape needs a small driver, hidden in the cabinet wall or pantry. The couch sconces plug in.
  • Couch: two plug-in scissor-arm sconces, one on the bank wall and one on the cabinet wall over the other arm (outlet R8, set in the pantry's side panel), each with its cord in a metal cover. The outlet beside the couch (R2) is a four-outlet box for the sconce, chargers and a lamp.
  • Bath: your two clear-glass sconces (LT13, LT14) go in now on the bank wall either side of a 15" vanity mirror, shades facing down, with its switch (S5) on the bath face of the cabinet wall just inside where the door will be. Nothing is installed on the bath's own future walls. Two boxes with blank covers are roughed in (F1 in the ceiling centred over the shower, for a shower light and fan; F2 for the vanity outlet) so the rest can be wired later without opening finished walls.
  • Kitchen: the working light is 12 or 24-volt LED tape (about 200 lumens per foot) in a channel under the head trim of each kitchen window, hidden behind a small lip and shining down onto the counter and bar. Its thin low-voltage feed tucks behind the window trim, so nothing has to pass through the solid header. The same tape, in a lower output, runs under both shelves. All the kitchen tape is on one driver, inside the sink base cabinet, and one dimmer at the back door. The beam strips have their own dimmer at the slider. The globe pendant at the window post is removed. LED strips on top of the two cross beams bounce light off the ceiling.
  • Entry: the sconce above the red door on the inside is removed.
  • Loft: one hardwired light on the bank wall near the top of the ladder, with two switches: one at the foot of the ladder on the main floor (S6) and one beside the bed (S3). It is on its own circuit and can be dropped later. An outlet above each bedside shelf takes a plug-in swing-arm lamp. The dome lights are switched at the slider only.

The porch-wall counter and bar have three GFCI outlets mounted sideways in the narrow strip between the counter and the window sills (K8, K9, K4), so no point on the counter is more than 2' from one. Draft, not a permit drawingSpacing follows common residential practice (an outlet within 6' along usable wall, GFCI at counters and outdoors).
